# Welcome to PAMPAS

This documentation refers to the driverless system of the Brazilian FSAE electrical team [Ampera Racing](https://amperaracing.com/) of the [Federal University of Santa Catarina](https://ufsc.br/).

**PAMPAS** stands for **P**rototype **AMP** **A**utonomous Driving **S**ystem. The **AMP** part refers to the name of our electrical protoype, which in turn comes from **Amp**era, the name of the team. [The Pampas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pampas) are also a grasslands ecoregion that covers part of sourthen Brazil.
