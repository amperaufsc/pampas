# First steps

This page is dedicated for new members of the Ampera Electrical FSAE team that are part of the Driverless subteam.

Here you're going to find the material you should study during your training process. You don't need to master the contents linked here, but it's important you get a good grasp of the basics, and as such the resources here are at the introductory.

The sections are ordered in (my) recommended order of study and divided in weeks. Each 

For the exercises, there is a base repository in our GitHub organization page that you can fork, clone and work on.

## Linux

Complete the getting started nad Command line chapters of the Grasshopper section of the [Linux Journey](https://labex.io/linuxjourney) guide.

## Week 1: CS50

Complete the first 6 weeks of the [CS50 Hardvard](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) introduction course to computer science. This course includes an intro to Python.

## Git

Chapters 1, 2 and 6 form the [Pro Git](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2) book. Chapter 6 is also recommended.

From the Main level, at least the Introduction Sequence and Ramping Up sections; and from the Remote tab at least the Push & Pull -- Git Remotes! section from the [Learn Git Branching](https://learngitbranching.js.org/) guide.

## ROS

Read the About ROS and complete the Get started section of the [ROS 2 official documentation](https://docs.ros.org/en/lyrical/First-Steps.html).
